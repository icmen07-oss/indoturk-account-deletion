IndoTurk Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your IndoTurk account and associated personal data using either method below.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open IndoTurk and sign in.
  2. Go to your account settings.
  3. Select the account deletion option and follow the instructions.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access your account, email us at icmen07@gmail.com with the subject "IndoTurk Account Deletion".

Include the email address associated with your account so we can identify it and verify your request. Never send your password.

What data is deleted?

When your account deletion is completed, your account and associated personal data are deleted in accordance with our deletion process. This may include your profile, profile photos, preferences, likes, matches, and associated messages.

Certain records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes. Any retained data is kept only as permitted and for the applicable retention period.

How long does deletion take?

We will review your request and process it after verifying your identity. If you submit your request by email, we will contact you if additional information is needed.

IndoTurk Hesap Silme

IndoTurk hesabınızın ve ilişkili kişisel verilerinizin silinmesini uygulamadaki hesap silme seçeneğinden talep edebilir veya icmen07@gmail.com adresine e-posta gönderebilirsiniz. E-postanızda hesabınızla ilişkili e-posta adresini belirtin; şifrenizi göndermeyin.

Silme işlemi tamamlandığında hesabınız ve ilişkili verileriniz, silme sürecimiz doğrultusunda kaldırılır. Yasal yükümlülükler veya meşru güvenlik ve dolandırıcılığı önleme amaçları nedeniyle bazı kayıtlar izin verilen süre boyunca saklanabilir.

Penghapusan Akun IndoTurk

Anda dapat meminta penghapusan akun IndoTurk dan data pribadi terkait melalui opsi penghapusan akun di aplikasi atau dengan mengirim email ke icmen07@gmail.com. Cantumkan alamat email akun Anda. Jangan pernah mengirimkan kata sandi.

Setelah penghapusan selesai, akun dan data terkait akan dihapus sesuai proses penghapusan kami. Catatan tertentu dapat disimpan jika diwajibkan oleh hukum atau untuk tujuan keamanan dan pencegahan penipuan yang sah, selama periode yang diizinkan.
